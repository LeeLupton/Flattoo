Aftercare

The first day
Leave the wrap on for the time your artist told you — usually two to four hours, longer for a second-skin film. Wash your hands, then wash the tattoo gently with lukewarm water and a mild, unscented soap. Pat dry with a clean paper towel; no rubbing, no cloth towels.

The first two weeks
A thin layer of the ointment or lotion you were given, two or three times a day — thin enough that the skin can breathe. Wash before every application. It will go through a tight, shiny stage, then peel and itch; that is normal. Do not pick, scratch or pull the flakes.

What to avoid
No soaking: baths, hot tubs, pools, the ocean, for at least two weeks. No direct sun and no tanning until it is fully healed; after that, sunscreen every time. No tight clothes rubbing on it, no gym on it for a few days, no pets sleeping on it.

When to call
Redness that spreads, heat, swelling that grows after day three, yellow or green discharge, a fever — call us the same day and see a doctor. Mild redness and clear fluid in the first two days are normal.

Later
A tattoo heals on the surface in about two weeks and under the skin in about a month. Touch-ups are looked at after six weeks. Moisturize and keep it out of the sun and it will stay sharp for decades.
