Ask Flattoo by name ("install pinta", "get me spotify") or by need ("something like paint", "something for invoices", "edit a video", "video call"). It shows what fits, says whether it is already installed, warns if an app wants more memory or graphics than this laptop has, and asks before installing. A few minutes later it is in the menu.

What is on the list, roughly:

- **Drawing & photos:** Pinta, LazPaint, FontForge, darktable, RawTherapee, Shotwell, Upscayl (make a small photo bigger), Blender, Scribus (flyers, cards).
- **Office:** LibreOffice (Word/Excel files, consent forms, invoices), PDF Arranger, Calculator, Calendar, Thunderbird mail, Chromium.
- **Talk & media:** Signal, Zoom, VLC, Audacity, OBS (record the screen or a time-lapse), Shotcut and Kdenlive (video), Spotify.
- **Tools:** Disk Usage Analyzer, Disks, KeePassXC, unit converter, Flameshot screenshots.

"What apps do I have" lists what is installed. "Remove blender" removes something you added; the base apps stay. "Put inkscape on the panel" adds a button.

## Not on the list?

Ask Lee. Anything can be added to the list; it is kept short on purpose so that what is there works well on this laptop.
