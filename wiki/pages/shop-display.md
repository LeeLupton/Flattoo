Open **Shop Display** from the menu, or tell Flattoo "shop display on". From then on, after the laptop has sat idle for the usual few minutes, the screen shows your portfolio one piece at a time instead of going dark. A tap on the touchpad brings the desktop back.

"Shop display off" (or the same menu entry) makes an idle laptop go dark again.

## Which pictures?

The newest gallery **Portfolio** built. **Choose a different folder…** in the same dialog points it at any folder of jpg, png or webp files instead — a hand-picked set for the front desk, for example.

## Keep in mind

- Anyone walking past sees the pictures. Keep it **off** when client photos that are not meant for the wall are in the folder.
- The laptop does not lock while the display is on. For a laptop that is handed over, use [Show a Client](show-client.html) instead — that one locks when it ends.
- The lid-closed lock and the sleep settings are unchanged.
