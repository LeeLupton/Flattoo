Tell Flattoo *"get paid 150"*, *"take a 50 deposit from Maya"*, or tap **Get paid** in Money.

1. **The amount.** Type it on the number pad, or tap $50 / $100 / $150 / $200. Pick what it is for — *Deposit*, *Balance*, *Tip*, *Flash* — and who, if they are on the books (leave it on *Walk-in* otherwise).
2. **Show the pay code.** The screen turns into a big code with the amount above it. Turn the laptop round. The client opens their phone camera, points it at the code, and their payment app opens with the amount and your name filled in. If you have more than one app set up, the tabs above the code switch between them.
3. **Paid ✓.** When it lands on your phone, tap *Paid ✓ Venmo* (or whichever). That is what records it — the laptop never asks the payment app, it trusts you. *Paid cash ✓* and *Paid by card ✓* are there for everything else.

A deposit taken this way marks the booking as reserved, and the *Deposit owed* card goes away.

## Which apps

| App | What the code does |
|---|---|
| **Venmo** | Opens Venmo with the amount and a note (“Deposit · Maya”) filled in. |
| **Cash App** | Opens Cash App with the amount filled in. |
| **PayPal** | Opens PayPal.me with the amount filled in. |
| **Square** | Opens your Square checkout page; the client types the amount. |
| **Zelle** | Zelle has no pay link. The code carries your number or e-mail as text; the client sends from their banking app. |

Set them up once under **Money › Settings** (or Lee does it in the system config): your Venmo username, your $cashtag, your PayPal.me name, a Square checkout link, your Zelle number. The handles stay on the laptop; nothing is linked, nothing logs in.

## No apps set up yet?

The feed shows a *How do you get paid?* card until at least one is. Cash and card still record in the meantime.
