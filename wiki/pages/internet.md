## Connecting

Click the Wi-Fi icon at the right of the panel (or ask Flattoo "wifi"), pick the network, type its password once. The laptop remembers it.

## Café and hotel Wi-Fi

Some networks show a login page first. Open Firefox; it notices and offers to open the page, or go to any website and you are sent there. Once logged in, everything works. The laptop steps its protections down only while that page is needed and steps them back up on its own.

## Firefox

Ad blocking is on. Firefox only opens secure (https) sites; on the rare plain http site it asks first — **Continue to HTTP site** is fine for a site you trust. Nothing is sent to Mozilla about what you do.

## No internet

1. Wi-Fi icon: are you connected to the right network?
2. Is the printer's Wi-Fi the one you joined by mistake? Some printers make their own.
3. Ask Flattoo "no internet" to see what it sees.
4. Turn Wi-Fi off and on from the same icon.
