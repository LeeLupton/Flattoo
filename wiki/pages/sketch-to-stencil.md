## 1. Get the sketch in

- **Scanner:** open **Document Scanner** (on the panel), press Scan. The scan lands in **Documents**. Scan at 300 dpi or more if the scanner asks.
- **Phone photo:** take the picture in even light, square to the paper, and send it over with [LocalSend](phone-photos.html). Photos work; scans work better.

## 2. Clean Lines

In **Files**, right-click the scan → **Clean Lines (Sketch to Stencil)**. It separates pencil from paper and writes a new file next to it, named like the original with `-stencil` at the end. Grey shading becomes black line; smudges and paper texture go away. Your original is untouched.

If the result loses light lines, redo the scan a little darker; if it keeps speckles, retake the photo in flatter light.

## 3. Print

Right-click the `-stencil` file → **Print as Stencil**, type the width in inches, Print. [Print as Stencil](print-stencil.html) explains the dialog.

The **Sketch to Stencil** layout opens the scanner and Files side by side for exactly this. [Layouts](layouts.html).
