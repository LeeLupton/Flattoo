Three things, all in the menu and in Ask Flattoo. They are offers: each one asks, shows what it will do, and does nothing on its own.

## Portfolio

**Portfolio** (or ask Flattoo *"make a portfolio"*) looks through Pictures, Downloads, Desktop and Documents for finished-work photos — big enough to be real pieces, not screenshots or stencil scans, no duplicates — shows you the list to untick anything, and builds a folder in **Portfolio/today's date**:

- **web/** full-size copies for a website or email
- **square/** 1080 × 1080 crops, ready for posts
- **index.html** a gallery page you can open on the laptop to show someone
- **contact-sheet.pdf** to print or send
- a small name mark in the corner if you want one

The rules it keeps, every time: your originals are not touched or moved; every copy has its camera, date and **location** information removed; nothing is uploaded or posted — the folder is yours to post from; and it reminds you to include only clients who agreed to be shown.

## Artist Profile

**Artist Profile** (or *"my resume"*) keeps the facts in one place — name, studio, city, since when, styles and skills, certifications with their expiry dates, awards and features, where to find you — and writes from them, into **Documents/Artist Profile**:

- **resume.html** and **resume.docx** (opens in Word or LibreOffice) for conventions, guest spots, studio applications
- **bio.txt** to paste into Instagram, a website, a convention form

Ask Flattoo *"add fine line to my skills"* and it is added; *"my resume"* renders it again.

## Exposure checklist

The free places a studio is found — Google Business Profile, Instagram, the website gallery, review sites, Facebook, convention calendars — with how long since you last refreshed each one and why it matters. Pick one when you have updated it; it marks the date. Ask Flattoo *"how do I get more clients"* or *"exposure"*.

Nothing is looked up or scraped: the laptop does not watch your accounts, cannot see your reviews, and never posts. It keeps the list and the dates you tell it.

## The weekly nudge

Monday mid-morning, one notification if there is something worth a minute: a certification that expires within two months, a listing not refreshed in six weeks, a dozen new photos since the last gallery, an empty profile. It says; it never does. Turn it off: Lee sets `flattoo.assist.reminders = false`.
