1. Turn the printer on and make sure Bluetooth is on (most Phomemo units blink blue when ready).
2. Open **Add Bluetooth Stencil Printer** from the menu, or ask Flattoo "bluetooth printer".
3. It scans and lists what it sees. Pick the printer.
4. If the printer asks for a PIN, it is in the printer's manual — usually `0000` or `1234`. Type it.

Done: the **Stencil** printer now prints over Bluetooth. Print as Stencil works exactly as before.

## Good to know

- Bluetooth is slower than the cable; a sheet takes a little longer to start.
- Keep the printer within a few meters and switched on; if it went to sleep, turn it off and on and print again.
- Plugging the cable in later switches back to USB automatically.
