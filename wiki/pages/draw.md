| App | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **Krita** | The main one. Pressure, stabilizers, mirror tools, layers, and it prints at true size. The closest thing to Procreate on a laptop. |
| **MyPaint** | Loose sketching: an endless canvas and very good pencils, nothing in the way. |
| **AzPainter** | Light and quick painting with proper pressure. The nearest to Paint that still works for real art. |
| **Rnote** | Pen notes and quick sketches, handwriting, a whiteboard. |
| **Inkscape** | Lettering, logos, anything made of clean lines you want to scale without blur. Text on a curve lives here. |
| **Xournal++** | Tracing: open a photo or PDF, draw over it on a layer, hide the photo. |
| **GIMP** | Photo fixing — levels, curves, retouching, G'MIC filters. In the menu, not on the panel. |

Ask Flattoo for more: "something like paint", "photoshop", "vector program", "app to make the photo bigger" — it shows what fits and installs it. [Installing apps](install-apps.html).

## Tablets

Plug the tablet in. Wacom and most Huion, XP-Pen and Gaomon tablets work on the spot, pressure included. A tablet with its own screen is mapped to that screen automatically.

If the pen draws on the wrong screen, unplug and plug the tablet back in. If pressure is flat in Krita: **Settings → Configure Krita → Tablet settings**, and check the pressure curve.

## Fonts for lettering

**Font Manager** in the menu shows every font on the laptop; a script set is already installed (League Script, Chorus, Cinzel, Old Standard, Garamond and more). To add a font, open the `.ttf` or `.otf` file from Files and click **Install**. Krita and Inkscape see it right away.
