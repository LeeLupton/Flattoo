Open **Looks** from the menu, or just tell Flattoo: "the neon look", "sakura", "dark mode", "light mode", "bigger text". A look applies instantly, nothing to restart.

| Look | For |
|---|---|
| **Home** | The default. Like a phone: app icons on the screen, a dock at the bottom, time and battery along the top, one tap opens things. Apps have familiar names — Files, Photos, Scanner, Documents, Spreadsheets, Mail, Messages, Settings — and familiar icons. The Super key shows every app as a grid. |
| **Home (dark)** | The same, dark, for evening sessions. |
| **Studio Dark** | Dark grey, cool accents, panel on top. Easy on the eyes under studio lights. |
| **Paper** | Light and warm, like the sketchbook. Daylight shops. |
| **Night Session** | Near-black; the panel hides until the mouse touches the bottom edge. Nothing but the canvas. |
| **Big and Simple** | Large text and icons, a dock at the bottom with the four things that matter: Krita, scan, calibration, Firefox. For a 4K screen or tired eyes. |
| **Classic Desktop** | Taskbar at the bottom, menu on the left, clock on the right, familiar names and icons. Feels like the last computer. |
| **Gruvbox** | Warm retro colors, panel on the left edge to keep the canvas wide. |
| **Blackwork** | Black, white and grey, halftone shading on the wallpaper, a dock at the bottom. For people who like their desktop like their linework. |
| **Flash Sheet** | Parchment with a draft grid and the traditional palette — red, mustard, bottle green, black. One panel on top like a desk. Becomes Blackwork at night. |
| **Neon** | A dark studio wall with neon tubes, hot pink and cyan. The dock runs down the left side; windows that are not in front go a touch see-through. |
| **Sakura** | Soft rosy paper and petals, round corners everywhere. Becomes **Sakura Night** in the evening: plum sky, a moon, a warmer screen. |
| **Forest** | Misty ridgelines in green-grey, soft contrast, one panel at the bottom with a window list. Calm, for long sessions. |
| **Kanagawa** | Deep water blues, foam-white text, a gold accent, waves on the wallpaper. |

Every look keeps **Ask Flattoo** one tap away and the same shortcuts. Each one also sets its own [window effects](effects.html) (rounded corners, shadows, blur), the colour of notification bubbles, and the app grid and [power sheet](shortcuts.html).

## Dark in the evening, by itself

Looks come in pairs: Home and Home Dark, Sakura and Sakura Night, Flash Sheet and Blackwork, Paper and Night Session, Classic Desktop and Studio Dark. Turn on the [automatic night look](night-look.html) and the dark twin takes over at the time you choose, the light one comes back in the morning. "Dark mode" said to Flattoo switches to the twin right now.

## Make it yours

Change anything by hand — **Settings → Appearance** for colors and fonts, **Window Manager** for title bars, right-click the panel for its size and buttons, right-click the desktop for the wallpaper. Then open Looks and choose **Save current look**. It appears in the list with the others.

## Going back

Open Looks and pick **Home**. Everything a look controls goes back to the default — including the app names and the icons on the screen.

## Keeping it

When the desktop is exactly right, [Lock This Setup](lock-setup.html) makes it stay that way.
