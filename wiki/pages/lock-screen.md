## Sleep and wake

Close the lid: the laptop sleeps and locks. Open it and the lock screen asks for your password — the **disk passphrase**, unless Lee set a different one at install. The power button also puts it to sleep.

## Shutting down

**Super + Escape** opens the power sheet — Lock, Sleep, Restart, Shut down, Log out, in big letters; so does the power icon at the right end of the top bar, or asking Flattoo "turn off". Restart, shut down and log out ask once more before they go. Hold the power button for ten seconds only if the screen is frozen solid.

## Battery

The battery icon on the right of the panel shows the charge; hover for the time left. Ask Flattoo "battery" for the same. The laptop dims the screen and slows down a little on battery to last longer; plugged in, full speed.

## Screen brightness

The sun keys on the keyboard (hold **Fn** if they do nothing). Ask Flattoo "screen too dark" for the display settings, including a second screen or projector.
