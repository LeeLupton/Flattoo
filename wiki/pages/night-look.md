Open **Looks** and choose **Automatic night look…**, or tell Flattoo: "dark mode at 9pm", "night look from 20:30 to 7", "make it dark in the evening". Turn it off with "stop the automatic night look".

What happens at the evening time:

- **Looks with a dark twin switch over.** Home → Home Dark, Sakura → Sakura Night, Flash Sheet → Blackwork, Paper → Night Session, Classic Desktop → Studio Dark. Everything changes together: colours, wallpaper, bubbles, the app grid.
- **Looks that are already dark** (Neon, Forest, Kanagawa, Gruvbox…) keep their colours and just get a **warmer screen** — less blue light, like a phone's night shift.

In the morning the day look comes back, screen colours go back to normal.

## Picking a look at night

Choosing a look by hand still works at any time. A look you pick during the day becomes the new day look; the schedule keeps working with its twin.

## The warm screen on its own

Sakura Night, Home Dark and Night Session always warm the screen a little, schedule or not. Any other look leaves the colours exactly as they are — the right choice while judging colours on a reference photo.
