**Files** is on the dock (or **Super+E**). On the left: **Home**, **Pictures**, **Documents**, **Downloads**, and any USB stick, SD card or phone that is plugged in.

Where things go by default:

- Scans → **Documents**
- Phone photos sent with LocalSend, and anything from the browser → **Downloads**
- Krita saves where you tell it; **Pictures** is a good habit.

## Right-click

Right-click a picture or PDF:

- **Print as Stencil** — [true size on the stencil printer](print-stencil.html).
- **Clean Lines (Sketch to Stencil)** — [pencil scan to black lines](sketch-to-stencil.html).
- **Open in Krita**.

## USB sticks

Plug it in; it appears on the left in Files and opens with a click. Before pulling it out, click the eject arrow next to its name. Zip files open by double-clicking.

## Finding something

The search box at the top-right of Files searches the folder you are in. The menu's search box finds apps, not files.
