Tell Flattoo *"leave a review"* or *"show the booking code"*, or swipe to the **Leave a review** / **Book the next one** cards in Money. The screen fills with a code; turn the laptop round and the client scans it with their phone camera.

- **Leave a review** opens your Google review page (or whichever link is set). Ask while they are still happy and still in the chair — that is when reviews happen.
- **Book the next one** opens your booking page. Good for a sleeve: book the next session before they leave.

The links are set under **Money › Settings** (the booking link falls back to the one on your Artist Profile). Lee can set them in the system too. Nothing is sent, counted or tracked — it is a picture of a link.
