Open **Layouts** from the menu or press **Super+L**. Pick one; the apps open and land where they belong.

| Layout | What opens |
|---|---|
| **Drawing** | Krita, full screen. |
| **Reference + Draw** | Krita on the left, the image viewer on the right. Drop the reference photo into the viewer. |
| **Sketch to Stencil** | Document Scanner on the left, Files on the right — scan, then right-click the scan for Clean Lines and Print as Stencil. |
| **Web Reference** | Krita on the left, Firefox on the right. |
| **Paperwork** | Firefox on the left, Files on the right. |

If an app is already open, the layout moves it instead of opening a second one.

## Your own layout

Arrange windows the way you like, then **Layouts → Save current layout** and give it a name. It remembers which apps and where.

## Moving windows by hand

Drag a window to the left or right edge of the screen and it snaps to that half. Drag it to the top to maximize. Double-click the title bar to maximize or restore.
