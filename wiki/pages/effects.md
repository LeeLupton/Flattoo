Windows have rounded corners and a soft shadow, the dock and notification bubbles blur what is behind them, and windows zoom in a little when they open. Every look decides how much of this it wants — Blackwork keeps corners nearly square, Sakura makes everything round, Big and Simple has none of it.

## Turning effects off

Say "effects off" to Flattoo, or open **Looks** and choose **Window effects**. The desktop goes flat and quick; nothing else changes. "Effects on" brings them back with the current look's settings.

Turn them off when:

- the laptop is old or runs on battery all day and the fan is loud,
- a window stutters while you draw (Krita full-screen is already left alone by the effects),
- a second screen flickers.

## Still slow?

Effects are not the usual reason a laptop feels slow. See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.html) — a full disk or a very long uptime are more likely, and Flattoo fixes both when asked.
