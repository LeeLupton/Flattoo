## LocalSend (no cable, no cloud)

1. Install **LocalSend** on the phone (App Store or Play Store, free).
2. Phone and laptop on the same Wi-Fi.
3. Open LocalSend on both. On the phone, pick the photos, tap Send, choose the laptop. On the laptop, accept.

The files land in **Downloads**. Nothing goes through the internet.

## iPhone by cable

Plug it in. The phone asks **Trust This Computer?** — tap Trust and enter the phone's passcode. A moment later the iPhone appears in **Files** on the left; the photos are under **DCIM**. Copy what you need to Pictures.

## Android by cable

Plug it in and, on the phone, choose **File transfer** (sometimes "MTP") in the USB notification. The phone appears in Files.

## Formats

iPhone HEIC, Android WebP and AVIF, and everything else open directly in the image viewer, Krita and Print as Stencil. No converting.
