Right-click the design in **Files** → **Print as Stencil**, or press **Super+P**, or open it from Krita with **File → Print** and choose the **Stencil** printer.

The dialog:

- **Width on skin (inches)** — the finished size. 3.5 means the design comes out 3.5 inches wide, exactly. Leave it blank to print the file at its own size.
- **Mirror** — No, normally. Stencil paper flips the image twice (carbon onto the top sheet, top sheet onto skin), so what prints is what goes on. Choose Yes only for a design that must read mirrored when it comes off the printer.
- **Lines** — **Crisp black lines** for a stencil; **Keep shading** when you want the greys (reference prints, not stencils).
- **Copies** — how many sheets.

Press **Print**. The stencil printer feeds and prints; a few seconds per sheet.

## Checking the size

Measure the printed design with a ruler. If it is off, print the [calibration page](calibrate.html) — it has a 4-inch bar and tells you what to do.

## If it is too big for the paper

The dialog says so and offers to shrink it to fit. Say no if the size matters more than fitting on one sheet; print it in two parts instead.

## No stencil printer around

Print as Stencil also offers the office printer, at true size on plain paper. Trace from that onto stencil paper with a thermal copier, or use it as a size check.
