## Turning it on

1. Press the power button.
2. Type the **disk passphrase** and press Enter. It is the only thing to remember; it also unlocks the screen.
3. The desktop appears, logged in as **artist**. No second password.

Close the lid to put it to sleep. Open the lid, type the passphrase, carry on where you left off.

## The desktop

The bar along the top is the **panel**. From left to right:

- **Menu** — every app, by category, with a search box. The Super key (the one with the window logo) opens it too.
- **Ask Flattoo** — the speech bubble. Type what you need in plain words. See [Ask Flattoo](ask-flattoo.html).
- **Krita, MyPaint, Inkscape** — drawing. See [Drawing](draw.html).
- **Files**, **Document Scanner**, **Stencil Printer Calibration**, **LocalSend**, **Firefox**.
- On the right: open windows, volume, battery, clock.

Right-click any picture in Files and you get **Print as Stencil**, **Clean Lines**, and **Open in Krita**.

## If you do not like how it looks

Open **Looks** from the menu (or ask Flattoo for "dark mode" or "light mode"). Six complete looks; pick one and it changes instantly. [Looks](looks.html).

## If something is wrong

Ask Flattoo first: "the printer isn't working", "no wifi", "free up space". If that does not sort it, [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.html), then Lee.
