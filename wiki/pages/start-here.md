## Turning it on

1. Press the power button. The Flattoo mark comes up on a dark screen.
2. Type the **disk passphrase** into the box on that screen and press Enter. It is the only thing to remember; it also unlocks the screen later.
3. The desktop appears, logged in as **artist**. No second password.

Close the lid to put it to sleep. Open the lid, type the passphrase, carry on where you left off.

## The desktop

It works like a phone. The icons on the screen open apps with one tap. The **dock** along the bottom has the ones you use most — the app grid, Files, Photos, Krita, Firefox, Ask Flattoo — and whatever is open. The strip along the top shows the time, Wi-Fi, sound and battery, and the power button at the right end (Lock, Sleep, Restart, Shut down).

- **The grid button** (or the Super key, the one with the window logo) shows every app as icons. Type a few letters to find one.
- **Ask Flattoo** — the speech bubble. Tap what you need from the list, or say it in plain words. See [Ask Flattoo](ask-flattoo.html).
- **Krita, MyPaint, Inkscape** — drawing. See [Drawing](draw.html).
- **Files**, **Photos**, **Scanner**, **Print Stencil**, **Calibration**, **LocalSend**, **Firefox**.

Right-click any picture in Files and you get **Print as Stencil**, **Clean Lines**, and **Open in Krita**.

## If you do not like how it looks

Open **Looks** from the menu, or tell Flattoo "dark mode", "the neon look", "sakura". Fifteen complete looks; pick one and it changes instantly, and it can go dark by itself in the evening. [Looks](looks.html).

## A client wants to see your work

**Show a Client** in the menu (or "show a client my work" to Flattoo) fills the screen with your portfolio and locks the laptop when they hand it back. [Show a client](show-client.html).

## If something is wrong

Ask Flattoo first: "the printer isn't working", "no wifi", "free up space". If that does not sort it, [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.html), then Lee.
