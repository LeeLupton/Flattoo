Click **Stencil Printer Calibration** on the panel (or ask Flattoo "prints too small"). It asks before using a sheet, then prints a page with:

- a **4.00 in bar** — measure it with a ruler;
- a grid in inches and centimeters;
- a letter **R** — if it reads correctly, nothing is mirrored.

## Reading it

- Bar is **4.00 in**: everything is right. Print stencils with confidence.
- Bar is a little short or long (3.9 or 4.1): the printer's paper feed is off. Tell Lee the measurement; it is a one-line fix in the setup and it lands overnight.
- The **R** is backwards: the design was printed mirrored. Check the Mirror setting in Print as Stencil — it should be No for stencil paper.

Do this once when the printer is new, and again if you change paper brand.
