Plug the printer into a USB port and turn it on. Within a few seconds the laptop sets up a printer called **Stencil** with the right paper size and settings. Print as Stencil uses it automatically.

Printers that work this way:

- **Brother PocketJet** — PJ-722, PJ-723 (the one in the Brother S8 stencil kit), PJ-762, PJ-763, PJ-773, and the PJ-8 series.
- **Phomemo / QuYin** — M08F and the M08F Plus/Pro/WS/S, TP81 to TP88, T08FS and most of their other thermal printers.
- Most other USB printers appear too, for plain paper.

## If nothing appears

Wait ten seconds, then ask Flattoo "the printer isn't working" or click **Fix Stencil Printer** in the menu. It looks again over USB, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth and tells you what it found. [Fix the printer](fix-printer.html).

No cable? [Bluetooth](printer-bluetooth.html) or [Wi-Fi](printer-wifi.html).
