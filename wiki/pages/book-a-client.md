Tap **Book** in Money, or say it: *"book Maya tomorrow at 2 for a dragon"*, *"schedule Ana for Tuesday 3pm"*, *"walk-in"*. Flattoo fills in what it heard; you check it and tap **Book it**.

1. **Who** — the name. Names you have booked before complete themselves.
2. **What** — a few words: *Dragon, forearm, black and grey*.
3. **Price** — type it or tap $150 / $250 / $400 / $600. Leave it empty if it is not settled yet.
4. **Deposit** — *None*, $50, $100, $150, $200. The usual one is already selected.
5. **When** — a day on the calendar, a time from the grid (half hours, your shop hours).
6. **Book it.**

If there is a deposit, the **pay code** comes up straight away with the amount filled in — take it while the client is standing there. [Get paid](get-paid.html). Until it lands, the booking shows on the feed as *Deposit owed*.

## On the day

The booking is the **Next up** card: *Consent form*, *Print stencil*, *Get paid* for the balance, and **Done ✓** when the chair is empty. *Done* is what starts the *Due to rebook* clock: a month later, if nothing else is booked for them, the feed reminds you.

## Changing or cancelling

A booking in the coming days with no deposit has *Cancel booking* on its card. Everything else: Lee can fix it, or just book it again — the old one does no harm.

## It is not a calendar app

There is no sync, no invitations, no account. Bookings live on this laptop, in Money. If you also keep a booking page online, its link can be shown as a code for clients to scan ([Book the next one](review-code.html)).
