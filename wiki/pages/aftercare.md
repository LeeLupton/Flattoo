Tell Flattoo *"print aftercare"* or *"aftercare"*, or **Papers › Print aftercare** in Money. One page: the studio name, your name and handle, then the instructions — the first day, the first two weeks, what to avoid, when to call, later. It also saves as **Documents › Business › Aftercare.pdf** for showing on the screen or sending.

## Changing the words

The text is a plain file Lee keeps with the system (`aftercare.txt`); say what you want changed and it changes for every sheet from then on. The studio name and handle come from your [Artist Profile](assist.html).
