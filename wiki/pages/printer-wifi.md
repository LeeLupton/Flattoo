If the printer is on the same Wi-Fi as the laptop, it is usually found on its own: Brother PocketJet 8 and office printers announce themselves; Wi-Fi Phomemo units are found by a quick sweep of the network.

If it is not there:

1. Open **Add Network Stencil Printer** from the menu, or ask Flattoo "wifi printer".
2. It lists the printers it can see. Pick yours.
3. If it is not in the list, choose **Enter an address** and type the printer's IP address (it is on the printer's status page or in its app), then pick the model.

The **Stencil** printer then prints over the Wi-Fi.

## Printing from the café

The laptop has to be on the same Wi-Fi as the printer. On a different network, use the cable or [Bluetooth](printer-bluetooth.html).
