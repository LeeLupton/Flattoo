Open **Lock This Setup** from the menu, or ask Flattoo "lock this setup". It explains what it will do and asks. Then it takes a minute to build.

After locking:

- The desktop you had — look, panel, layouts, shortcuts, right-click actions, Krita settings — is **restored at every login**. Change something by accident, log out and back in, it is back.
- Panel, desktop and session settings need the administrator password to change.
- The screen locks after ten idle minutes.
- Firefox will not install new add-ons.
- The laptop keeps getting **security updates** every week but **stops taking setup changes from the internet**. Only what is on the laptop counts.

You can still draw, print, scan, install apps through Ask Flattoo, and use everything as before.

## Changing your mind

**Unlock Setup** in the menu asks for the administrator password (Lee's). After unlocking, your look stays as the default, but you can change things again and setup changes from Lee arrive overnight again.

## When to lock

When you have not wanted to change anything for a week or two. There is no hurry; locking is for making a good setup stay good.
