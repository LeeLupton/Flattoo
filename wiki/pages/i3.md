Most people never need this. If you like every window to have its own space and your hands on the keyboard, there is a second session:

1. Log out (menu → power icon → Log Out).
2. On the login screen, click the session icon and pick **i3**.
3. Log in.

Windows open in tiles that fill the screen. **Super+D** opens a launcher — type the first letters of an app, Enter. **Super+Space** is Ask Flattoo, same as always. **Super+1** to **Super+5** switch workspaces; **Super+Shift+1…5** move the window there. **Super+Shift+Q** closes a window, **Super+F** makes it full screen, **Super+Shift+F** lets it float.

Everything else — Print as Stencil (**Super+P**), Layouts (**Super+L**), the printers, the files — is the same laptop.

To go back to the normal desktop, log out and pick **Xfce Session** at the login screen.
