During a consultation, open **Show a Client** from the menu, or tell Flattoo "show a client my work". After one confirmation your portfolio fills the screen:

- **tap** or the right arrow — next piece; right-click or the left arrow — back; the wheel zooms,
- pieces also move along by themselves every few seconds,
- notifications stay quiet,
- **Esc** ends it — and **locks the laptop**. Your password brings the desktop back.

Nothing can be deleted, moved or saved from the slideshow, and there is no way out of it except the lock screen. Hand the laptop over without worrying.

## Which pictures?

The newest gallery **Portfolio** built (`~/Portfolio/<date>/web` — the copies with phone data removed). If there is none yet, Flattoo offers to build one from your finished-work photos first. [More about Portfolio](assist.html).
