Open **Money** (the green card on the dock, or tell Flattoo *"who's next"*, *"today's take"*). It opens on the **feed**: one card fills the screen, swipe up (or scroll, or the arrow keys) for the next.

## The cards, top to bottom

- **Next up** — the next client today: their name, the piece, the time, what they have paid and what is owed. The big button is whatever the money needs next: *Get deposit*, *Get paid $300*, or just *Get paid*. Under it: *Consent form*, *Print stencil*, *Done ✓*.
- **Later today** — the rest of today, one card each.
- **Deposit owed** — a booking in the coming days with no deposit yet. The slot is not reserved until it lands; *Show the pay code* is the whole fix.
- **Today** — what came in today, with this week, this month and this year under it.
- **Coming up** — the next three bookings.
- **Due to rebook** — a client you finished a month or more ago with nothing booked. Healed by now; one tap books the next session.
- **Leave a review**, **Book the next one** — codes the client scans with their phone, if the links are set.
- **Aftercare sheet** — print one for every client who walks out.

Nothing on a card happens by itself. The feed says; you tap.

## The tabs

| Tab | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Get paid** | Number pad → a code the client scans → *Paid ✓*. [Get paid](get-paid.html) |
| **Book** | Who, what, when, price, deposit. Six taps. [Book a client](book-a-client.html) |
| **Papers** | The consent form and the aftercare sheet. [Consent form](consent-form.html), [Aftercare](aftercare.html) |
| **Money** | Today, this week, this month, this year; every payment; *Export for taxes*. |

## For the accountant

Every payment is also written to **Documents › Business › Money › ledger-2026.csv** — date, amount, how it was paid, deposit or balance or tip, the client. The file is rewritten each time, so it is always current; *Export for taxes* on the Money tab just tells you where it is. Open it in any spreadsheet.

## The morning note

Once a day, mid-morning, a notification: how many clients today and when, deposits owed, clients due to rebook. It never books, charges or sends anything. Lee can change the time or turn it off.

## Where things live

Bookings, payments and client details stay on this laptop — nothing is uploaded, no account anywhere. The consent forms and the ledger are plain files in **Documents › Business**; they are yours and they go wherever your Documents go.
